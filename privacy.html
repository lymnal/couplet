What Couplet stores

The short version: your parlor code is the only key, and there is no account attached to any of it.

What's kept

Whatever the two of you put in: the names you typed, your game progress and streaks, your Four Things entries, your Inklings answers, the notes on the wall, and the photo if you added one. All of it is stored under your parlor code and nothing else.

What isn't

  • No accounts. No email, no password, no sign-up — there's nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, trackers, ads, or cookies. Nobody is measuring you.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. There is no one to sell it to.

The honest part about the code

Your parlor code works like a key under the mat: anyone who has it can read and change everything in your parlor — the notes, the photo, all of it. That's the trade for having no accounts. Treat it like a password: send it to your person and nobody else, and be careful screenshotting a screen with the code showing at the top.

If a code gets out, the fix is to start a fresh parlor with a new code. Everything you make there is separate.

Who can see it

Whoever has the code. Also, unavoidably, the database host (Supabase) and the static host (GitHub Pages), who keep ordinary server logs. The person who built Couplet can technically reach the database, and doesn't go looking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Deleting it

In your parlor, scroll to the bottom and choose delete parlor. It asks you to type the code, then erases everything for both of you, immediately and permanently. There is no backup to restore from and no undo. You can also remove a single note or the photo without deleting the rest.

Children

Couplet isn't intended for anyone under 13.

If this changes

Couplet is a small project made for two people at a time, not a company. If any of the above changes, this page changes with it — it lives in the open source repository, so its history is public. Questions or a takedown request can be raised as an issue there.
